FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE New Fast Payment System will interoperate with existing RTGS, ACH, and Sand Dollar CBDC infrastructure — a first-of-its-kind integration in the Caribbean Nassau, Bahamas /May 05, 2026 /Montran/ — The Central Bank of The Bahamas has selected Montran to design and implement a nationwide Fast Payment System (FPS), enabling real-time, near 24/7 domestic payments for individuals, businesses, and government entities. The system is expected to go live in Q1 2027.
