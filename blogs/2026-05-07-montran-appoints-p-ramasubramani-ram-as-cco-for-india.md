FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE Veteran payments and BFSI leader to drive growth across India and support cross-regional expansion into MENA, APAC, and Africa Mumbai, India /May 07, 2026 /PRNewswire/ — Montran, a global provider of payment and capital market infrastructure solutions with operations in over 90 countries, has appointed P. Ramasubramani (Ram) as Chief Commercial Officer (CCO) for India. Ram brings over two decades of experience across payments technology, financial services, and the broader BFSI sector.
