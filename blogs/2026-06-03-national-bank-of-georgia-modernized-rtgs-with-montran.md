The National Bank of Georgia and Montran successfully launched an upgraded Real-Time Gross Settlement platform supporting ISO 20022 messaging and 24/7 processing, enhancing resilience, scalability, and operational efficiency for Georgia's payment infrastructure. The collaboration also includes implementing an Instant Payments System expected to launch by year-end 2026.
