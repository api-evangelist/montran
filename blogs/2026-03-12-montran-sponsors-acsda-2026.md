<p><strong>Montran Sponsors ACSDA 2026 </strong></p>
<p>Montran will be participating as a sponsor <strong>of the ACSDA – Americas’ Central Securities Depositories Association General Assembly</strong>, a key event that brings together leaders from across the capital markets ecosystem to discuss industry developments and the future of financial market infrastructures in the region.</p>
<p>The <strong>ACSDA 2026 General Assembly</strong> will take place in <strong>São Paulo, Brazil from March 18 to 20, 2026</strong>, gathering central securities depositories, financial institutions, and technology providers to exchange perspectives on current market challenges and opportunities.</p>
<p>As part of the event program<strong>, Miguel Espinoza, Head of Capital Markets at Montran</strong>, will participate in the panel discussion:</p>
<blockquote><p><strong>Evolution of Technology Platforms: Current Trends </strong></p></blockquote>
<p>The session will explore how evolving technology platforms are supporting the modernization of capital markets infrastructure. Discussions will focus on the growing need for scalable systems, integrated platforms, and automation to enhance operational efficiency and resilience across post-trade environments.</p>
<p><strong>Mauricio Trujillo, Deputy General Manager, Montran LATAM,</strong> will also participate as a panelist in the session:</p>
<blockquote><p><strong>Application of Artificial Intelligence in Central Depository Processes and Its Impact on Market Infrastructure: Scale vs. Subscale</strong></p></blockquote>
<p>This panel will examine how artificial intelligence is being applied within central securities depository operations and the potential impact on market infrastructure. The discussion will address opportunities for improving efficiency, risk management, and decision-making, while also considering the challenges and scalability implications of implementing AI across different market sizes.</p>
<p>With solutions designed to support <strong>post-trade platforms (CSD), investor portals, and integrated capital markets systems,</strong> Montran continues to help market infrastructures modernize their operations and adapt to the evolving demands of global financial markets.</p>
<p><strong>Let’s connect!</strong></p>
<p>We look forward to connecting with industry peers and contributing to the conversations shaping the next generation of capital markets technology.</p>
